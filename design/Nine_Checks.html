The Nine Checks: Does This Deserve to Be Built?

Timed Funding Outreach Engine · Rohit, accelerator program recruitment · updated for the current build
UPDATED wording changed since the first version

⚡ Kill Switches

Any No means: don't build it.

01 Problem real? YES

Rohit misses 30–40% of announcements by hand and spends 20 minutes researching each startup. The 30–60 day window after a raise is when outreach converts best.

02 Workflow repeated? YESUPDATED

Every few hours the same steps run: scan the news, spot a raise, research, judge fit, write the email. Identical for every startup. It runs on a schedule (every 3 hours) and on demand.

03 Input available? YESUPDATED

Funding news is public and live (9 RSS feeds; Gemini Flash with Google Search only to find a startup's website). The thesis is edited by Rohit in the dashboard's Settings. Founder emails are rarely in articles, so we use Hunter.io (verified addresses only), then addresses the startup publishes on its own site, marked unverified. If neither works, the draft is made with the recipient blank and flagged.

📊 Sizing

Any No means: build something smaller.

04 Output valuable? YESUPDATED

A ready, personalised draft inside the 30–60 day window is directly a recruitment lead for the accelerator. A Telegram message tells Rohit the moment new drafts are ready.

05 Impact measurable? YESUPDATED

Announcements caught vs missed, hours from announcement to draft, minutes per outreach (20 today), drafts approved. The dashboard logs it from day one: every article and what happened to it (Sources), every run (Runs & health), and the approval tabs (Drafts), against Rohit's own 30–40% miss rate.

08 ROI worth it? YESUPDATED

Saves about 20 minutes per fit startup and recovers the 30–40% now missed. Running cost is low: Vercel, Supabase, GitHub Actions, Telegram and Hunter on free tiers; Gemini Flash pay-per-use with limits per run.

🚧 Boundary

Any No means: put a human exactly here.

06 Failure risk OK? YES for drafting, NO for sendingUPDATED

A wrong draft costs nothing because nothing is sent. An email to a founder speaks for the accelerator and cannot be recalled, so Rohit sends. A draft deleted by accident can be restored from the dashboard.

07 Judgment protected? PARTLYUPDATED

Extracting, scoring and drafting are assistive. Setting the thesis (Settings), approving and sending, and replying to founders stay with Rohit. The access code guards every change; the Telegram reminder only nudges and never acts.

09 Owner clear? YES

The system owns detection through drafting and the reminder. Rohit owns the thesis, every send and the founder relationship after that.

Verdict: build it, draft-only. Automate find, assess, personalise, remind. A human approves and sends.
